## Algorithm and Dosing Changes

- **Round basals to pump's actual supported rates**
  - Basal recommendations are now rounded against the actual paired pump's supported rates.
  - The pump is handed a bit-exact rate to avoid tiny numeric errors lowering the delivered dose.
  - An obsolete stored pump model setting was removed so the app no longer falls back to a bundled default.
  - This prevents unintended small drops in delivered basal rates for affected pumps.
  - [View source](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio/pull/1429)
- **Fix DynamicISF crash when autosens min is 1**
  - Fixes a crash when DynamicISF uses the sigmoid option and autosens minimum is set to 1.
  - The sigmoid calculation now nudges the minimum value so sensitivity ratios adjust with glucose instead of causing an -∞.
  - As a result, Swift's oref behavior differs from the JavaScript version for this specific edge case.
  - [View source](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio/pull/1443)
- **Dosing Mode replaces Closed Loop toggle**
  - The Closed Loop toggle in Settings is replaced by a Dosing Mode picker.
  - Only Closed and Open modes are offered in Settings right now, with short explanatory hints.
  - Your existing Closed Loop setting is migrated automatically: true → Closed, false → Open, unknown → Open.
  - The Manual Temp Basal screen and related controls have been removed.
  - Onboarding now pre-fills Max IOB to one third of your total daily basal instead of leaving it at zero.
  - [View source](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio/pull/1504)
- **Add Low Glucose Suspend and Basal Testing modes**
  - Adds two new dosing modes: Low Glucose Suspend and Basal Testing.
  - Low Glucose Suspend treats your Max IOB as 0 so Trio will not add corrective insulin.
  - Basal Testing fixes sensitivity and disables dynamic ISF so you can observe your programmed basals, and only suspends \<.
  - Entering Basal Testing cancels any Trio-set temp basal and Trio will otherwise recommend no temp basals.
  - These modes are temporary overrides that do not modify your saved preferences, so your settings are restored when you退出.
  - [View source](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio/pull/1506)
- **Show algorithm's open-loop bolus suggestion**
  - The bolus screen now shows the algorithm's open-loop bolus suggestion.
  - That suggested amount is reduced by your Recommended Bolus Percentage and capped by Max Bolus.
  - The suggestion is hidden when the algorithm asks for a basal reduction instead of a bolus.
  - Open loop still does not deliver the suggested bolus; this only displays the recommendation.
  - [View source](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio/pull/1509)
